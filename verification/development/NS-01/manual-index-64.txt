1.5.1. Convert to NSC Group
Converts a range of surfaces in the Lens Data Editor into either:
• A group of 3D objects in a Non-sequential Component (NSC) surface for ray tracing in mixed mode. See
"Non-Sequential Component" for more information on the NSC surface and see "NSC ray tracing in mixed mode"
for more information on NSC ray tracing in mixed mode.-or-
• A new system in non-sequential mode. See " NSC ray tracing in non-sequential mode " for more information on
NSC ray tracing in non-sequential mode.
If converting to a mixed mode system, itis recommended that you insert dummy surfaces before and afterthe range
of surfaces you wish to convert, and then choose these as the first and last surfaces forthe conversion. If converting
to a "pure" non-sequential system, dummy surfaces are not required.
