3.4.3.11. Phase vs. Wavelength (coatings, polarization and surface physics
group)
For sequential surfaces, this feature computes the S and P polarization phase for reflection (if the glass is MIRROR)
orfortransmission (ifthe glass is not MIRROR) as a function of incident wavelength. For non-sequential objects,this
feature computes the S and P polarization phase for reflection (if the material is MIRROR) or for transmission (if the
material is not MIRROR) as a function of incident wavelength.
