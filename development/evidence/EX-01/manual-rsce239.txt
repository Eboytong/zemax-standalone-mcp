Discussion:
Operands are set by typing the name in the first column and then filling in the remaining data fields. There are
multiple fields that may be required to define an operand. The fields are called Int1 and Int2 for the two integer
values, and Data1 through Data6 for up to 6 double precision values. Not all of the operands use all of the fields
provided.
