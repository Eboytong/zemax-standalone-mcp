2.3.1.2.59. Paraxial XY
The paraxial XY surface type is similar to the paraxial surface except the optical power can be specified in the X and
Y directions separately. This surface can therefore be used as a paraxial cylindrical ortoroidal lens. Two parameters
are required to define the paraxial XY surface: the X power, and the Y power. OPD through paraxial XY surfaces is
computed using mode = 1 described in the paraxial surface description.Optical poweris measured in inverse length
lens units, such as inverse millimeters if the lens units are millimeters. See "Units".
The paraxial XY surface shape is plane.
