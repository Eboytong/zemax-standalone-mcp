3.4.1.2. Polarization Pupil Map
Generates a graph of the polarization ellipse as a function of pupil position. This aids in visualizing the change in
polarization over the pupil. This feature may also be used to compute transmission in systems modeled using
interfering paths in separate configurations, such as birefringent polarizing beam splitters and interferometers.
