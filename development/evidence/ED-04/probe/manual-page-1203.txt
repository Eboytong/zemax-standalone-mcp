Settings:
Surfaces The lens surfaces to match. Choose All or Variables. If All is chosen, all glass elements with glass surfaces
are matched. If Variables is chosen, all glass elements that have variable radius status are matched.
Vendor(s) Choose all or specific stock lens vendors.
Show Matches The maximum number of stock lens matches to show.
EFL (Effective Focal Length)Tolerance (%) The maximum EFL tolerance that matched stock lenses can deviate
from the defined lens. This value is the EFL difference divided by the EFL of the defined lens expressed as a percent.
EPD(EntrancePupilDiameter)Tolerance (%) The maximum EPDtolerance that matched stock lenses can deviate
from the diameter ofthe defined lens. The EPDof matched lenseswill not be smallerthan the diameter ofthe defined
lens. This value is the EPD difference divided by the diameter of the defined lens expressed as a percent.
AirThickness Compensation If selected, air thicknesses allocated as variables will be reoptimized after the stock
lens is in place using the specified number of damped least squares (DLS) cycles.
#Opt.Cycles The number of cycles ofDLS to use when optimizing airthicknesses. This option is only available when
air thickness compensation is enabled.
Match Shape This option is selected by default. If selected, the Stock Lens Matching Tool will use a lens that has a
shape factor close to the original design. If unselected, the Stock Lens Matching Tool will try replacements lenses
regardless of their shape factors.
MatchOrientationThis option is selected by default. If selected,the Stock LensMatching toolwill match the defined
lens orientation of the lens to be replaced. If unselected, the Stock Lens Matching Tool will check replacements
lenses in both orientations.
The orientation of the lens corresponds to its stored configuration in our lens vendore library database. When the
"Match orientation" option is selected, the product of the lens shape and the stock shape is negative, that means
the two shape factors have a different sign. If the user is flexible with lens orientation and open to using an inverted
vendor lens, the stock lens orientation will first be reversed before further considering it for substitution in the
design.If the original lens shape factor is exactly zero, or if the product of the lens shape and the stock shape is
positive,the lensmatching algorithmwillnotflipthe stock lens.Afterrunning the Stock LensMatchingTool algorithm,
