This feature calculates the surface phase as a cross section across the full clear diameter or diameter of the surface.
This feature defines phase slope in units of 2p radians perlens units. TheGraph section will display (0,0) atthe vertex
of the aperture, including for off-axis apertures. The Text data X and Y coordinates are given with respect to the
vertex of the surface.
Surfaces which do not impart a phase change to the ray, such as the Standard surface, will display a phase of zero
everywhere on the surface phase display.
