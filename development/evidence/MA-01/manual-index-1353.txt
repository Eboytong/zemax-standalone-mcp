Data" for more details.
If the minimum and maximum wavelength inputs are both zero, then the left X-axis of the plot will be determined
by the minimum wavelength for which data are available for any of the input glasses (even if other glasses do not
have data at this wavelength) and the right X-axis of the plot will be determined by the maximum wavelength for
which data are available for any of the input glasses (even if other glasses do not have data at this wavelength).
